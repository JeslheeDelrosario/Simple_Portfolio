Hello! I'm John Jeslhee M. Del Rosario, a recent graduate from Bulacan State University with a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics specializing in Computer Science. Based in Malolos, Bulacan, I'm actively seeking full-time developer roles where I can contribute to building scalable, user-centric web applications. I bring strong analytical foundations paired with hands-on full-stack development experience to every project I take on.

With a solid track record of delivering production-ready applications, I specialize in building responsive frontend interfaces using React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, while developing robust backend systems with Node.js, Express, and PostgreSQL. My technical toolkit also includes modern DevOps practices like Docker, API integrations with multiple AI providers, and authentication systems (Keycloak SSO) – skills I've applied to solve real-world problems in academic and professional settings.

Explore my portfolio to see standout projects like TISA, an AI-powered learning management system I built for my university's College of Science, featuring context-aware AI tutoring, real-time notifications, and role-based access control. I also developed ListaGo, a comprehensive task management app used by dozens of users to organize their workflows, and contributed to enterprise solutions like a payroll automation system that streamlined processing for hundreds of employees.

What sets me apart is my unique blend of mathematical rigor and practical development experience – I don't just write code; I architect solutions that are efficient, maintainable, and deliver real value. I'm eager to bring this mindset to a forward-thinking team, continue growing my skills, and help build products that make a difference. If you're looking for a dedicated developer who cares about code quality and user experience, let's connect to discuss how I can contribute to your next project.
